About Private.chat

Private.chat is a self-hosted, end-to-end encrypted chat application.

  • Direct Messaging: Chat directly with your Google contacts using WebSockets
  • End-to-End Encryption: Messages are encrypted client-side and only participants can read them
  • WebPush Notifications: Get notified when friends message you or send friend requests
  • Google Sign-In: Secure authentication with Google OAuth
  • No Room IDs: Direct chat with friends, no room codes needed
